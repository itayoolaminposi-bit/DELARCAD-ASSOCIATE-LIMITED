ABOUT US

Twelve people, three studios, one drawing standard.

We stayed small on purpose. Every project gets a principal's attention from the first sketch to the final punch list.

How we work together

Delarcad Associate Limited is organised as three studios — Lagos, London and Toronto — that share one set of drawing standards and one review process. A project based in Lagos can pull a structural detail refined by the Toronto studio without a translation step, because the standard is the same everywhere.

We keep teams to three people per project: a principal, a project architect and a technical lead. No project is handed to a rotating cast of juniors partway through construction documentation.

What we value

  • Site firstEvery scheme starts from a site visit, not a floor plan template.
  • Buildable drawingsA drawing set is only finished when a contractor can bid it without calling us to ask what we meant.
  • Local materialWe specify what the region actually produces well, before we specify what looks good in a rendering.
  • Direct contactClients speak with the architect on their project, not an account manager relaying messages.

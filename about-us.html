ABOUT US

A multidisciplinary company, mainly for clients' joy.

Delarcad Associate Limited is a Nigerian company operating across architectural design, construction, project management, property development and real estate.

Company overview

We provide integrated solutions for clients seeking to transform ideas, land and development opportunities into functional, aesthetically refined and valuable properties.

Our approach combines architectural thinking with practical construction and development considerations — from conceptualization and site assessment through design development, documentation, construction coordination and completion.

We are positioned to undertake projects across the residential, commercial, institutional, recreational and property-development sectors, collaborating with the appropriate professionals and specialist consultants each project requires.

Vision

To build a respected and trusted Nigerian design, construction and property-development company recognized for architectural excellence, quality project delivery, professional integrity and enduring value creation.

Mission

To transform our clients’ ideas and development opportunities into well-designed, properly coordinated and purposeful spaces — giving appropriate consideration to function, aesthetics, safety, constructability, cost and long-term value.

PROPOSED FLAGSHIP DEVELOPMENT

A 2,000-capacity ultra-modern event centre

We have developed a conceptual framework for a proposed 2,000-capacity ultra-modern event centre, demonstrating our capacity to conceptualize and coordinate large-scale commercial and recreational developments. The facility can be adapted to suit a client's objectives, available site, planning requirements and final technical design.

Subject to land availability, feasibility studies and planning considerations, potential high-profile Lagos locations include Victoria Island, Lekki, Ikoyi and other strategic commercial corridors. The final location would be determined through proper site assessment, land due diligence, planning analysis, accessibility considerations and development feasibility.

Delarcad Associate Limited is prepared to support the Chartered Institute of Directors (CIoD) Nigeria in the development of its proposed infrastructure initiatives, including this conceptual event centre and other future projects.

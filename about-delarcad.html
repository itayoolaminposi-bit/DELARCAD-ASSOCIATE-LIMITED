THE PRACTICE

Delarcad Associate Limited

Founded in 2011 in Lagos, now practicing across West Africa, the UK and North America.

Our history

Delarcad opened as a two-person studio in Lagos in 2011, working primarily on residential commissions. The practice's first public building, the Odiwe Civic Library, opened in 2013 and remains one of the most visited civic buildings in its district.

A London studio followed in 2016 to support a growing number of UK-based residential and conservation projects, and a Toronto studio opened in 2020 to lead commercial and mixed-use work in Canada. Today the three studios operate as one practice, sharing a single drawing standard and review process.

Our mission

To design and deliver buildings that serve the people who use them for decades, not the photograph taken on opening day. We aim to be the practice that clients call again for their second and third building — because the first one still works exactly as intended.

Registration & standing

Delarcad Associate Limited is a registered architecture practice with licensed architects of record in Nigeria, the United Kingdom and Canada. We partner with a local architect of record in any jurisdiction outside these three.

REPRESENTATIVE WORK

Projects that shaped how we work

2013

Odiwe Civic Library, Lagos

Our first public building, and the project that set the practice's approach to natural ventilation in West African civic architecture.

2018

Thornhill Terrace Conservation, London

Restoration and rear extension of a Grade II listed terrace, delivered without altering a single sightline from the conservation area.

2022

Quaybridge Commercial Row, London

A five-unit mixed-use development built to Passivhaus performance targets on a constrained urban infill site.

2023

Marrowbend House, Toronto

A private residence built around an existing stand of white pine, which the client asked us to design around rather than remove.

Common questions

  • Do you work outside Lagos, London and Toronto?Yes — we've delivered projects across four countries by partnering with a licensed architect of record in each jurisdiction.
  • What's the smallest project you'll take on?We generally start at single-residence new-build or major renovation. Smaller interior-only projects are considered case by case.
  • How long does a typical house take?From first meeting to move-in, most custom homes run 18–30 months, depending on approvals and site complexity.
  • Do you provide construction administration?Yes. We stay engaged through construction rather than handing off a finished drawing set and stepping away.

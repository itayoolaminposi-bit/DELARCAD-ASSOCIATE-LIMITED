Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we work, before you send a brief.

  • What services do you offer?Architectural design, design & build, building construction, project management & construction supervision, property development, land & real estate services, and building materials supply.
  • Which sectors do you work in?Residential, commercial, hospitality & recreation, institutional and property-development sectors, across Nigeria.
  • Do you handle land sourcing and title verification?Yes — land and property sourcing, site identification, acquisition support and development-suitability assessment are part of our land & real estate services. All transactions are subject to appropriate title verification, searches and due diligence.
  • Can you manage a project from design through construction?Yes — our Design & Build service coordinates concept, design, documentation, approvals, procurement, construction, supervision, completion and handover as one process.
  • Do you supply building materials?Yes, we facilitate coordinated procurement and supply of selected building materials according to a project's agreed specifications and requirements.
  • Do you work with other professionals on a project?Yes — depending on scale and nature, we coordinate with structural, civil, mechanical and electrical engineers, quantity surveyors, surveyors, town planners and other specialists as required, with registered professionals engaged wherever statutory sign-off is needed.
  • Where are you based?Our offices are in Lagos and Abuja, Nigeria.

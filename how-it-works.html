HOW IT WORKS

From first call to move-in

Six stages, the same for every project regardless of studio or scale.

01

Discovery Call

A 30-minute conversation about your site, program and budget range. We'll tell you honestly whether Delarcad is the right practice for the project — no charge, no obligation.

02

Site Visit & Feasibility

A principal visits the site, reviews zoning and surveys, and returns a short feasibility memo covering what's realistic on your site and budget before any design work begins.

03

Concept Design

Two to three design directions, presented as plans, sections and a physical or digital massing model. You choose one direction to develop, or ask us to combine elements of several.

04

Design Development

The chosen concept is resolved into detailed plans, structural coordination, material selections and, where relevant, interior layouts — reviewed with you at each milestone.

05

Construction Documentation & Permitting

A full construction drawing set, coordinated with structural and services engineers, submitted for permit and issued to bidding contractors.

06

Construction Administration

Regular site visits, contractor RFIs and change-order review through to final walkthrough — the same team that designed the project sees it built.

2–4 Weeks for feasibility & concept
4–8 Months for design development & documentation
12–22 Months typical construction period

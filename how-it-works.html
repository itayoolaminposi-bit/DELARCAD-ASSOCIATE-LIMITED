HOW IT WORKS

Our project delivery approach

Successful construction begins with proper planning. Our methodology is structured around eight stages.

01

Client Brief

We begin by understanding the client's vision, requirements, intended use, budget considerations and development objectives.

02

Site & Feasibility

We assess the site and consider its characteristics, opportunities, limitations and development potential.

03

Concept Development

We develop initial concepts that respond to the client's brief and the opportunities presented by the site.

04

Design Development

The preferred concept is developed into a more detailed architectural and technical proposal.

05

Technical Coordination & Approvals

Relevant professional consultants and authorities are coordinated as required by the project.

06

Procurement & Construction Planning

Construction requirements, materials, contractors, scheduling and project logistics are considered.

07

Construction & Supervision

The approved project is implemented with appropriate coordination, supervision and quality monitoring.

08

Completion & Handover

The completed development is reviewed, outstanding works are addressed and the project is prepared for handover.
